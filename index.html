Containment failure as a single narrative spine

AI Security Engineering, Agent Risk Governance, and Vulnerability Standards

Three 2026 incidents are the same failure class at three severity levels. This course follows that spine from the SDL gates that should have caught it, through the telemetry that would have surfaced it, to the standards bodies now deciding where such a flaw even gets reported.

Ken Huang · OWASP AIVSS Project Lead · OWASP AST10 Project Lead · Co-Chair, CSA AI Safety Working Group
